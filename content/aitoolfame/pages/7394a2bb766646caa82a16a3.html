FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Why should I choose a paid plan?

Paid plans (Basic, Pro, Guest Post, Sponsor, and Directory Submissions) offer significant advantages: - **No badge required**: Keep your site clean and professional - **Faster listing**: Immediate or priority review queues - **More dofollow links**: Up to 5+ high-quality backlinks - **Better visibility**: Featured spots on homepage and categories (Pro+) - **Lifetime value**: One-time payment for permanent listing

What are the benefits of the Basic plan?

The Basic plan offers several benefits: - Get 3 dofollow links to boost your SEO - No badge verification required - Lifetime listing - Listed immediately - Customer support

What is included in the Directory Submissions plan?

With Directory Submissions, we submit your product to 100+ curated directories on your behalf: - **All Pro listing benefits** included ($19.9 USD value) - **Massive exposure**: We manually submit your product to 100+ high-quality directories - **Guaranteed results**: Increase your Domain Rating (DR) to 15+ - **Save time**: Save 20+ hours of tedious submission work - **Fast turnaround**: Completed within 96 hours with a full report

What is included in the Guest Post plan?

The Guest Post plan gives you a permanent article placement on ToolFame.com: - **All Pro listing benefits** included ($19.9 USD value) - **Your content**: You provide one finished, original article - **Quality backlinks**: Include up to 2 contextual dofollow links - **Editorial checks**: Quality and relevance review, light formatting, and basic on-page SEO checks are included - **Fast publishing**: Published within 7 days after editorial approval

Can I submit for free?

Yes, we offer a basic free listing for bootstrapped founders. **Note the requirements:** - **Mandatory Badge**: You must place our badge on your site permanently - **Slow Review**: Wait time up to 180 days - **Limited Exposure**: Normal listing style only - **Strict Monitoring**: Automated checks will remove your listing if the badge is missing For a professional presence without these restrictions, we recommend the Basic plan.

What are the differences between Basic and Pro plans?

Here are the key differences between Basic and Pro plans: Basic Plan: - Get 3 dofollow links to boost your SEO - No badge verification required - Lifetime listing - Listed immediately - Normal display style - No featured listing - Customer support Pro Plan: - All Basic listing benefits - Get 5 dofollow links to boost your SEO - Featured spot at homepage - Featured Filter at category pages - Featured display style - Customer support

How does the payment work for Basic and Pro plans?

Both Basic and Pro plans use a One Time payment model - no recurring fees or subscriptions! What this means: - Pay once - Keep your listing forever (lifetime listing) - No monthly or yearly charges - No automatic renewals - Complete ownership of your listing Why One Time payment? We believe in fair, transparent pricing. Pay once and enjoy permanent benefits without worrying about recurring costs! Note: Sponsor includes 30 days of promotion with no automatic renewal. All Pro listing benefits remain after the promotion ends.

What currencies do you accept for payment?

Our pricing is listed in US Dollars (USD), but we accept payments in multiple currencies! How it works: - All plan prices are displayed in USD - During checkout, you can pay with your preferred currency - Payment processor automatically converts at current exchange rates - You'll see the exact amount in your local currency before confirming Supported currencies include: EUR, GBP, CAD, AUD, JPY, and many others. The final amount will be equivalent to the USD price based on real-time exchange rates.

Do I need to provide a backlink for my listing?

For the Free plan, a backlink to our site is required. All paid plans include a permanent listing without any backlink requirement.

What happens if I remove the badge from my website?

🚨 IMPORTANT: For Free plan users, the badge must remain visible permanently on your website. Consequences of removing the badge: - We perform regular automated checks on all Free plan websites - If the badge is missing, your listing will be immediately unpublished - No warnings will be sent - removal equals instant delisting - You'll need to upgrade to a paid plan to get your listing back Want badge freedom? Upgrade to Basic or Pro plans - no badge requirements ever!

What are the benefits of the Sponsor plan?

The Sponsor plan is our premium tier offering maximum visibility: - All Pro listing benefits ($19.9 USD value), retained after the 30-day promotion ends - Top placement in homepage listings - Sponsor spot at all category pages - Sponsor spot at all detail pages - Sponsored display style during the 30-day promotion - Non-exclusive placement shared with other sponsors - Priority customer support Pricing: $59.9 for 30 days - One-time payment for 30 days of priority exposure.
