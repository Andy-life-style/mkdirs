Introduction

Mentor is an innovative AI coach designed to guide users toward self-discovery and personal growth by fostering introspection rather than providing direct answers. Unlike traditional chatbots that offer solutions, Mentor specializes in asking pertinent questions that help users uncover their own paths and insights. It operates on a unique principle: "My questions, your path," emphasizing user agency in their developmental journey.

A core differentiator of Mentor is its advanced long-term memory. While most AI assistants struggle with conversational continuity, Mentor is engineered to remember critical details—your goals, commitments, past statements, setbacks, and breakthroughs—verbatim across weeks and months. This deep contextual understanding allows the AI to ask highly relevant questions, much like a human coach who has followed your journey closely. This ensures that the guidance provided is always personalized and builds upon previous interactions, avoiding generic advice or compressed summaries that might lose nuance.

Key features and benefits of Mentor include:

  • AI Accountability Partner: Users can set specific goals and commitments, which Mentor tracks diligently. Instead of merely reminding you, the AI engages with you by asking the right questions at opportune moments, prompting reflection and action towards your objectives.
  • Actual Continuity with Long-Term Memory: The platform stores your conversational history, ensuring that your personal narrative and progress are consistently remembered. This enables a truly continuous coaching experience where past discussions inform future interactions, leading to more profound insights.
  • AI Journal that Writes Back: Mentor functions as an interactive journal. Users can freely express their thoughts and concerns, receiving thoughtful questions in return rather than prescriptive lectures or templated plans. This method encourages genuine self-exploration and helps users articulate their own solutions.
  • Anti-Addictive by Design: Consciously built to avoid common gamification tactics, Mentor eschews streaks, intrusive push notifications, or other addictive features. Its philosophy is that success means needing the app less, empowering users to become self-reliant.
  • Private Conversations: User privacy is paramount. Mentor is GDPR-compliant, ensuring that all conversations are kept confidential. User data is never used to train other models, and there's an option to delete all data at any time.

Mentor is explicitly not a replacement for ChatGPT or similar generative AI tools that provide direct answers. It is tailored for individuals seeking to delve deeper into their thoughts and find their own solutions through guided questioning. It is suitable for adults aged 18 and above, offering reflective coaching rather than therapy, and may not be appropriate for those in acute crisis.

Currently, Mentor is available as a private Android beta, offering a 1-month free trial with no commitment. Users can easily install the app from the Google Play Store and begin their trial immediately. The development team actively encourages user feedback to shape the future of the platform, ensuring it remains aligned with user needs and continues to foster genuine personal growth.
